This is a collection of projects for the course ELEC4260 (Intelligent Robots and Embodied AI) @ HKUST, 2025 Spring. Instructor: [Prof. Ping TAN](https://ece.hkust.edu.hk/pingtan).

## Project Overview

Develop and implement AI-driven robotics solutions for autonomous navigation and manipulation, based on vision-based techniques (***PnP localization***, ***visual odometry***, ***stereo vision***) and learning-based algorithms (***neural networks***, ***reinforcement learning***), sensor fusion (***EKF***, ***IMU***), path planning (***A\****), and grasp synthesis, to enable real-time robot control and environment interaction in lab and project settings.

The whole course's projects are divided into the following sub-projects.
